# macXenos 1.0.1 — release notes

macXenos is an unofficial macOS port of Xenia Canary with a native Metal
renderer. It runs Xbox 360 games on Apple Silicon Macs, and on Intel Macs
with an AMD GPU (Radeon RX 580 with 4 GB or better). It needs macOS 15 or
later. This is a fix and polish release; it follows 1.0 (2026-11-12).

## Install / update

If you have 1.0, choose **macXenos > Check for Updates…**, or wait for the
scheduled check (System > Software update in Preferences). Otherwise
download `macXenos-1.0.1.dmg`, open it and drag macXenos to Applications.
The app is a Universal app, signed and notarized by Apple. Your settings,
saves and game library carry over.

## What changed since 1.0

### Stability

- Quitting with the dashboard open no longer hangs, switching games no
  longer crashes, and Stop no longer takes the app down a few seconds
  later. A game started after Stop opens fresh.
- **Shadowrun** plays its matches: every mode used to drop out of its
  lobby after about ten seconds.
- **Worms Collection:** the launcher starts its games.
- **Xbox Live Arcade games** run as the full game instead of the trial
  ("Unlock Full Game").
- **Call of Duty: Ghosts:** the campaign finds its second disc when both
  discs are in the same folder.
- More accurate system behaviour, tested against a real Xbox 360. Small
  test programs ran on a console and on macXenos, and the results were
  compared step by step: thread waits, timers, locks, critical sections and
  text functions now match the console's answers in thousands of recorded
  steps, where 1.0 differed in hundreds of them.

### Graphics

- **Madden NFL 10, 13, 17 and 25:** no more huge flat polygons over the
  field and players. **Madden NFL 10**'s sky is no longer pink and blue
  noise.
- **Star Trek (2013)**, **Medal of Honor: Warfighter**, **Need for Speed:
  The Run**, **LEGO Indiana Jones 2**, **L.A. Noire** and **Call of Duty:
  Ghosts:** no more garbled textures (block noise, rainbow colours or
  polygon shards).
- **Gears of War 3:** Anya's face in the opening cutscene is no longer a
  blank white mask.
- **Call of Duty: Black Ops III:** the title screen shows its artwork
  instead of black.
- **Rise of the Tomb Raider:** no more white-outs or snow drawn as huge
  rings.
- **Project Gotham Racing 3**, **Batman: Arkham Asylum** and **Mirror's
  Edge** render at a true 2x internal resolution, not an enlarged 1x
  picture.
- Anti-aliased edges blend exactly as they do on the Xbox 360.

### Speed and memory

- **Mafia II** no longer hitches two or three times a second (uncapped on
  an M5 Max: 185 to 200 fps).
- A settings file brought over from an Intel Mac no longer keeps an Intel
  setting that slows Apple Silicon (Mafia II on an M5 Max: about 60 to 184
  fps).

### App

- The title bar and Preferences > About show the build number as well as
  the version.
- New FXAA anti-aliasing option (Graphics > Anti-aliasing) smooths jagged
  edges in any game.
- Games that never cap their own frame rate, like GTA IV and Mafia II, can
  be held at 120, 180, 240 or a custom rate (Graphics > Uncapped games).
- **Project Gotham Racing 3** and **4** play their race music.
- All Games sorts four ways (X: most or least recently played,
  alphabetical either way) and has a card view (Y). Both are remembered.
- Dashboard sounds are no longer cut off when you move through a list
  quickly.
- `.zar` disc images show up in All Games when you add a game folder.
- The play history no longer lists a game under the name and icon of one
  you stopped before it.
- The [compatibility list](../compatibility.md) covers 228 games, up from
  151, with M1 Max and M5 Max columns beside the two M4 Macs.

### Controllers

- Controller support moves to SDL3, the current version of the library
  macXenos uses for controllers.
- More controllers are recognised and rumble (for example the 8BitDo
  Ultimate 2 over Bluetooth). On Nintendo-layout controllers the face
  buttons now follow their position, as on an Xbox pad.
- Rumble stops while the dashboard is open over a game, the Vibration
  switch takes effect at once, and Controller > Vibration has a Test
  vibration button.
- Anti-dead zones for each stick (Off, 15%, 24%, 27% or custom) skip a
  game's own dead zone, and the dead zone now centres a drifting stick in
  every direction (before, only up or right).

## Known issues

- **Rise of the Tomb Raider:** a single false-colour frame can still flash
  by now and then on M4 and newer Macs.
- **Gears of War** and **Gears of War 2** at 2x or 3x: characters' shadows
  break into dark triangles and characters go too dark. 1x is clean.
- **Burnout Revenge:** menu and event videos flicker black above 1x.
- **Marvel vs. Capcom 2** can freeze while loading characters.
- **Supreme Commander 2** gets past its storage prompt now, but its main
  menu ignores the controller.
- **Madden NFL 06** stays on its loading splash.
- **Medal of Honor: Warfighter** can crash about 25 seconds into the first
  mission.
- **Need for Speed: The Run** sometimes stops during the opening race.
- **Forza Horizon 2:** audio can stutter when many sounds play at once.
- The 1.0 issues in **Fight Night Round 4**, **The Elder Scrolls V:
  Skyrim** and **Jurassic: The Hunted** remain.
- See the [compatibility list](../compatibility.md) for every tested game.
